Multi-Window & SplitSync Quick Guide

A high-performance standalone desktop terminal for multi-chart TradingView workflows:

  • Multi-Window (Ctrl+N): Open new independent windows. Move them across multiple physical monitors.
  • Cross-Window Crosshair Sync: Moving your mouse anywhere displays synchronized crosshairs and timestamps across all charts and screens in real-time.
  • Symbol Sync: Changing symbols in any window or via the Watchlist updates all synchronized charts simultaneously.
  • Custom Watchlist: Add your favorite tickers using the + button. Click any ticker to view, or click × to remove.
  • Drawings Cloud Sync (Ctrl+S): Instantly saves line tools, Fibonacci levels, and technical analysis to your TradingView account.
  • Edge Split-Screen: Smooth draggable splitters with 50/50 double-click reset and 2V, 2H, 3-Chart, 4-Grid, and 6-Grid presets.
  • Clean Mode / Max Space: Eliminates sidebars, promotional popups, and bottom panels to provide an unobstructed candlestick view.
